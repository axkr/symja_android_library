Contents: the reviewed (Swiss-Prot) human entries (organism 9606) annotated
with the Gene Ontology term GO:0003779 "actin binding" or one of its
descendants: the primary gene name, the recommended protein name, the
canonical sequence and the Gene Ontology annotations with their term names
as UniProt gives them. The Gene Ontology term names come from the Gene
Ontology (http://geneontology.org), CC BY 4.0.

Downloaded on 15 November 2026 from the UniProt REST API:

  https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=(organism_id:9606)+AND+(reviewed:true)+AND+(go:0003779)&fields=accession,gene_primary,protein_name,length,sequence,go_f,go_p,go_c&format=tsv
  https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/stream?query=(organism_id:9606)+AND+(reviewed:true)+AND+(go:0003779)&fields=accession,gene_primary,protein_name,xref_hgnc&format=json

Changes: the records were reduced to the fields above, the GO annotations
stored as indexes into a term table and the class names (camel-cased term
names) added. The table is written by matheclipse-chem/tools/gen_protein_data.py.
